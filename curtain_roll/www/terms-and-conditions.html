Section 01

Custom Manufacturing Protocol

Kayan Andalus Curtains operates an on-demand custom production facility in Riyadh. Every blind, motorized shade, and wavy drape is custom-cut, stitched, and assembled precisely to the width, height, fabric selection, and control side specified by the client.

In compliance with the E-Commerce Law issued by the Ministry of Commerce in Saudi Arabia, tailor-made products manufactured to bespoke consumer specifications are exempt from standard returns and cancellations once production cutting has commenced.

By confirming your order and payment, you authorize our atelier to immediately initiate fabric precision cutting based on your submitted specifications.

Section 02

Measurement Accuracy & Sizing Responsibilities

Achieving a perfect window fit requires careful consideration of mounting placement:

  • Inside Frame (Recess Mount): The client is responsible for providing the exact inside window frame width and height. Our automated manufacturing pipeline applies a standard 1.5 cm deduction across the top mechanism to ensure the roller blind operates freely without wall abrasion.
  • Outside Frame (Wall / Ceiling Mount): We manufacture the exact total fabric and mechanism width entered. Clients are advised to add at least 5 to 10 cm on each side to prevent light leaks around window frames.
  • Measurement Guidance: Our technical team provides live video guidance and laser meter assistance via WhatsApp (+966 11 455 0783) prior to order processing.
Section 03

5-Year Quality & Motorization Warranty

All products manufactured by Kayan Andalus are backed by our comprehensive warranty:

  • Motors & Smart Controls: 5-Year direct replacement warranty against internal mechanical failure, electronic defect, or factory motor malfunction (Somfy, Dooya, or Tuya ecosystems).
  • Mechanical Hardware: 3-Year warranty covering aluminum headrails, heavy-duty clutch chains, roller tubes, and brackets against structural deformation.
  • Fabrics: 2-Year warranty against premature UV discoloration and delamination under standard indoor residential exposure.
  • Exclusions: Warranty does not cover accidental fabric tearing, damage from corrosive cleaning chemicals, or unauthorized structural modifications.
Section 04

Cancellation, Replacements & Defect Claims

We are dedicated to ensuring your complete satisfaction with your window treatments:

  • Amendments Window: Orders may be amended or cancelled free of charge within 6 hours of order submission before automated cutting scripts are committed.
  • Manufacturing Defect Guarantee: In the rare event that an item arrives with a manufacturing defect, damaged mechanism, or size variance exceeding ±5mm from order specifications, Kayan Andalus will remanufacture and replace the product at zero cost to the client.
  • Filing a Claim: Defect notifications must be filed within 48 hours of parcel receipt by contacting 8001268000 or messaging our WhatsApp desk with clear photos and packaging tags.
Section 05

Delivery, Protective Transit & Inspection

We partner with premier Saudi logistics carriers (including SMSA and Aramex) to deliver custom blinds across all provinces of the Kingdom.

  • Transit Protection: Every curtain unit is encased in heavy-gauge rigid PVC tubes or reinforced corrugated packaging to prevent bending during long-distance domestic shipping.
  • Delivery Timeline: Custom manufacturing requires 3 to 6 business days, followed by 2 to 4 business days for courier transit to your doorstep.
  • Parcel Inspection: If outer transit packaging appears punctured or crushed upon arrival, clients should note this with the courier and notify us immediately.
Section 06

On-Site Installation Services

Where professional installation is added to your order:

  • Our installation technicians service major metropolitan sectors (including Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam).
  • The client must ensure the installation area is accessible, clear of furniture, and that electrical supply points are prepared for hardwired smart motorized blinds.
  • Our team will test all mechanical chain balances and remote-control smart motor limits prior to customer sign-off.
