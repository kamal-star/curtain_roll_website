Data Governance & Trust

Privacy Policy

How Kayan Andalus Curtains safeguards your identity, window specifications, property measurements, and payment data in strict accordance with Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

Policy Sections
  • 1. Information We Collect
  • 2. How Data is Used
  • 3. 3D Renders & Images
  • 4. Payment Security
  • 5. Third-Party Sharing
  • 6. Your Rights & Contacts
Section 01

Information We Collect

When you utilize the Kayan Andalus digital atelier, configure custom blinds, or book on-site laser measurements, we collect essential operational data:

  • Identity & Account Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, and email address registered through our platform.
  • Delivery & Property Addresses: National Address, city, neighborhood, and GPS geolocation coordinates used by our technicians and courier partners (e.g., SMSA, Aramex).
  • Technical Window Data: Custom width and height inputs, mounting preferences (inside vs. outside frame), control side orientations, motorization channels, and custom printed image uploads.
Section 02

How We Use Your Personal Data

Your data is processed strictly to fulfill manufacturing, logistics, and legal obligations within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

  • Automated Production: Passing millimeter dimensions directly to our automated fabric cutting machines and ERPNext production pipeline.
  • Technician Dispatch: Scheduling installation and measurement crews to your villa, apartment, or commercial office.
  • Transactional Notifications: Sending automated order confirmations, tax invoices compliant with ZATCA (Fatoora), and tracking updates via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Warranty Verification: Registering and validating serial numbers for your 5-year motor and hardware guarantees.
Section 03

3D Visualizer Canvas & Uploaded Images

Our interactive 3D configurator utilizes client-side WebGL canvas rendering. When you click "Add to Cart", a static snapshot of your configured shade in the room scene is captured to verify fabric and valance options for manufacturing verification.

Photos of windows or architectural facades uploaded by clients for consults are treated as strictly confidential. They are never shared publicly or used in promotional materials without explicit, written customer consent.
Section 04

Payment Security & Financial Privacy

Kayan Andalus does not store, process, or retain credit card numbers or banking CVVs on our servers. All financial transactions are encrypted through Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) certified payment gateways:

  • Card Processing: End-to-end tokenized processing via Mada, Visa, MasterCard, and Apple Pay using PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption.
  • Installment Providers: When choosing Tamara or Tabby, identity verification and installment underwriting are executed securely on their regulated platforms.
Section 05

Authorized Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is disclosed only to verified service partners strictly necessary for order fulfillment:

  • Domestic Courier Partners: Providing name, contact phone, and delivery address to logistics companies for parcel delivery.
  • Governmental Authorities: Disclosures mandated by royal decrees, judicial orders, or compliance regulations under the Saudi Ministry of Commerce or ZATCA.
Section 06

Your Rights & Data Governance Contact

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you maintain the right to access, review, update, or request the erasure of your personal records from our databases, subject to statutory tax record retention requirements.
