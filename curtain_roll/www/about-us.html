Our Philosophy

More Than Curtain — Architectural Precision for Every Space

At Kayan Andalus, we craft bespoke window shading solutions uniting millimeter-precision cutting, certified thermal barriers, and automated smart-home motorization.

100% Custom

Millimeter-Cut Precision Factory

Pioneering the Digital Blind Experience in Saudi Arabia

Founded to transform how homeowners and commercial architects dress windows, Kayan Andalus combines an interactive 3D digital studio with a precision manufacturing atelier in Riyadh.

From total blackout solutions resisting harsh gulf summers to glare-filtering sunscreens and luxury ripple-fold wavy draperies, every product is custom engineered to your exact window dimensions.

We work directly with European fabric mills and Somfy/Dooya smart motorized track providers to bring effortless automation, safety, and modern elegance directly to your living rooms and offices.

Automated Millimeter Cutting

Every blind is cut using computer-guided laser equipment, guaranteeing flawless symmetry, clean fray-free seams, and a tailored fit.

Thermal & Solar Protection

Engineered textiles that reflect solar UV rays and block ambient heat, visibly lowering indoor room temperatures and air-conditioning electricity bills.

Smart Home Ecosystems

Seamless wireless integration with Alexa, Google Home, and Tuya automation systems, with rechargeable battery and silent motor options.
