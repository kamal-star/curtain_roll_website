Section 01

Custom Production & Manufacturing Timelines

Because Kayan Andalus does not sell pre-cut generic curtains, every order undergoes specialized bespoke production:

  • Standard Manual Roller Blinds: 3 to 5 business days for precision cutting, fabric edge-sealing, and clutch mechanism assembly.
  • Motorized & Smart Home Systems: 4 to 6 business days to allow motor pairing, channel programming, and electrical testing under tension.
  • Specialty & Wavy Ripple-Fold Drapes: 5 to 7 business days for custom track bending, glider spacing, and hand-tailored bottom weighted hems.
Section 02

Domestic Shipping Coverage & Couriers

We partner with premier Saudi courier logistics (including SMSA Express and Aramex) to deliver to all cities, governorates, and remote towns across the Kingdom:

  • Major Hubs (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Mecca, Medina): Courier delivery within 2 to 3 business days once dispatched from our factory.
  • Other Regions & Provinces: Courier delivery within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Tracking: Automated tracking links are sent via SMS and WhatsApp once the package is registered with the carrier.
Section 03

Rigid Transit Packaging Standards

Window blinds contain long aluminum tubes and tracks that are vulnerable to bending if shipped in standard soft cartons. To eliminate transit damage, Kayan Andalus employs a specialized packaging system:

Blinds with widths exceeding 1.2 meters are encased in heavy-gauge industrial cylindrical PVC tubes or reinforced 5-ply triangular cardboard sleeves with impact-absorbing foam endcaps.

All mounting brackets, child safety tensioners, remote controls, and screws are enclosed in a separate sealed internal accessory kit.

Section 04

Professional On-Site Installation Service

For customers who select the With Installation option during customization (SR 80.00):

  • Coverage Cities: Our internal technical crew directly covers the Greater Riyadh Region, Jeddah, Mecca, Medina, and the Eastern Province (Dammam, Khobar, Dhahran).
  • Scheduling: Once your curtains are manufactured, our dispatch team will contact you via phone or WhatsApp to coordinate a convenient installation appointment.
  • Scope of Work: Mounting brackets to concrete, drywall (using heavy-duty anchors), or aluminum window frames, clipping the shade in place, setting top/bottom drop limits, and pairing remotes.
Section 05

Client Site Readiness Requirements

To ensure a smooth, professional installation experience, clients are kindly requested to ensure the following:

  • Clear Access: Furniture, desks, or beds beneath window frames should be moved back by at least 1 meter to allow ladder access.
  • Electrical Pre-wiring: If hardwired AC motors (220V) are ordered, electrical supply points must be installed by a certified electrician adjacent to the motor head prior to our arrival. (Rechargeable lithium battery motors require no pre-wiring).
  • Authorized Adult Present: An authorized adult representative must be present to confirm final drop heights and sign the handover inspection form.
Section 06

Transit Damage & Defect Claims Protocol

If you choose self-installation and your package arrives damaged:

  • Inspection upon Receipt: If the outer tube is visibly crushed, creased, or punctured, request that the delivery driver record the damage on the delivery note.
  • 48-Hour Reporting Window: Open your parcels and report any internal mechanism damage within 48 hours by calling our toll-free support line 8001268000 or messaging WhatsApp with photos.
  • Immediate Remanufacture: Any product proven to have sustained transit damage will be prioritized in our production schedule and replaced at zero additional cost to you.
